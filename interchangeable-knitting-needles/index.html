Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the difference between interchangeable and fixed needles?

Fixed needles are single sets, great for one style of knitting. Interchangeable systems use a core cable to which various sized tips can be attached, offering flexibility across different projects (e.g., going from small socks to large blankets) with minimal equipment change.

Do I need an interchangeable set for every yarn weight?

No. While versatility is nice, the best needle size ultimately depends on your preferred gauge and the specific project you are doing. Always check pattern recommendations first.

Are metal needles always better than wood needles for knitting?

It's a personal preference, but metal is often slicker on delicate yarns (like silk), while wood/bamboo provides more grip and warmth in the hand, which some knitters find comforting.

How do I clean my interchangeable needles?

Most modern systems are easy to clean. A gentle wash with mild soap and warm water is usually sufficient. Avoid harsh chemicals that can strip the finish or damage proprietary cables.
