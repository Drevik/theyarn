## Why Foam Rolling Matters

Foam rolling is a form of self-myofascial release that helps reduce muscle soreness and improve flexibility. A 2024 study in the Journal of Athletic Training found that foam rolling after exercise reduces delayed-onset muscle soreness by up to 40%.

## How to Choose

Consider density, size, and texture. Beginners should start with a medium-density smooth roller. Experienced users benefit from textured surfaces for deeper release. A 12 to 13-inch roller works for most people.
