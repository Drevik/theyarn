Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: July 22, 2026

In plain English: When you click a product link on The Yarn and buy something on Amazon, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This is how we keep the site running. Our recommendations are never influenced by how much a product pays us.

FTC Disclosure Compliance

This disclosure is provided in compliance with the Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255). The FTC requires that material connections between endorsers and the products they review be disclosed clearly and conspicuously.

In accordance with these guidelines:

  • This disclosure appears on every page of The Yarn that contains affiliate links (via the footer on each page)
  • The disclosure is placed before any product recommendations, not buried at the bottom of a page
  • The language used is clear and understandable — not hidden in legal jargon
  • We do not use ambiguous phrases like "links may earn us a commission" — we state plainly that we earn commissions when you purchase through our links

How We Earn Money

The Yarn participates in the Amazon Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

When you click on an affiliate link on our site and make a purchase on Amazon, we receive a small commission from Amazon. This commission is paid by Amazon — you never pay more for a product because of our affiliate relationship.

These commissions help us maintain the site, fund research, and continue producing free content.

Our Editorial Independence

Our affiliate relationships do not influence our product recommendations, ratings, or reviews. This is not just a promise — it's a principle we built the site around:

  • We research and evaluate products based on quality, value, and user satisfaction — not commission rates
  • We provide honest assessments of both pros and cons — no product is perfect
  • We never recommend products we wouldn't consider using ourselves
  • We update our guides when products change or better options become available
  • A product's affiliate commission rate has zero bearing on whether we recommend it or where it appears in our guides

If a product we've recommended is no longer the best option in its category, we update the guide — even if the replacement earns a lower commission. Trust is worth more than any single affiliate payout.

Where You'll See This Disclosure

We make our affiliate relationship clear in multiple places:

  • Every page footer: "As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases" appears in the footer of every page on the site
  • This disclosure page: Linked from every page's footer for full details
  • Product links: All product links that earn us a commission are affiliate links — there are no sponsored or paid placements

FTC Endorsement Guide Compliance

In accordance with the FTC's Endorsement Guides (updated 2023), we disclose:

  • The nature of our material connection to the products we review (affiliate commissions via Amazon Associates)
  • That our endorsements reflect our honest opinions, findings, or experiences with the products
  • That we are not compensated directly by product manufacturers — our income comes solely from Amazon Associates commissions when you make a qualifying purchase
  • That we have not received any free products in exchange for reviews (all product assessments are based on research, not free samples)
