Zone I: The Murky Vestibule (Entry Point & Tutorial)

  • Description: The initial entry point. Sunlight struggles to penetrate the deep water, casting eerie beams through particulate matter. Broken marble statues of forgotten scholars litter the floor, now coated in phosphorescent algae. The sound is a constant, muffled drip and groan from above.
  • Challenge Focus (Encounter 1): A group of mutated 'Brine Lurkers' (use standard Giant Constrictor Snake stats, but give them limited spell resistance). They are drawn to noise, making stealth the primary challenge for early-game success.
  • Key Interaction: The party must pass a skill check (e.g., Investigation DC 14) on an ornate, waterlogged sign that reveals the central mystery: the library wasn't just flooded—it was sealed by powerful magic and something is actively maintaining the pressure differential.

Zone II: The Corroded Archives (The Mystery & NPC Interaction)

  • Description: This area was once filled with thousands of shelved texts, now reduced to a chaotic field of decaying paper and rotted wood suspended in semi-liquid pockets. The air here smells strongly of salt and ozone. Visibility is poor.
  • NPC Encounter: Archivist Lyra. A desperate, semi-fae creature who was originally tasked with sealing the library. She is obsessed with the "Chronicles" but views trespassers as pollutants to be contained. (Roleplay hook: she offers a vital piece of information about the central vault's weakness in exchange for an impossible task).
  • Challenge Focus (Encounter 2): A puzzle/environmental combat mix. The players must stabilize three magically charged runestones before a localized magical surge (caused by Lyra) collapses a section of the archive, forcing movement and timing checks under pressure.

Zone III: The Nexus Vault (The Climax & Artifact Retrieval)

  • Description: The deepest, most powerful chamber. Here, the water is unnaturally calm and luminous blue. At the center sits a crystalline dais holding the "Chronicles of the Sundered Star," radiating unstable power. Massive, geometric magical constructs drift through the space.
  • Final Encounter (Encounter 3): The Keeper. This creature/entity was designed to protect knowledge at any cost. Use stats based on an Elder Brain, but give it a unique ability: "Knowledge Drain" (saps magic points or spell slots). The battle is won not by brute force, but by fulfilling the conditions set forth in the ancient texts—requiring teamwork and clever use of skills learned from Lyra.
  • Climax: Once the Chronicles are retrieved, the sealing magic fails catastrophically. The party must make a final escape roll (DC 16 Athletics or Survival) before the entire structure sinks around them.

💡 Master Tools & Tips for Running the Session

  • Plot Twist Example: The "Chronicles" aren't records of history—they are instructions on how to cause the next global cataclysm, and retrieving them forces a moral dilemma.
  • Loot Integration: Success in the Nexus Vault grants the party two unique items: e.g., Tome of Aetheric Stabilization (minor anti-magic effect) and Brinewalker's Cloak (advantage on Stealth checks in water).
