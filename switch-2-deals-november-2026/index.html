Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a regular microSD card in the Nintendo Switch 2?

No, and this is the most expensive mistake in the Switch 2 ecosystem. The console only accepts microSD Express cards for game storage, which use a PCIe-based data path instead of the older UHS-I bus. A standard microSD card physically fits in the slot but cannot hold Switch 2 games; Nintendo limits those older cards to viewing screenshots and videos. Express-compatible cards such as the Samsung P9 Express read at up to 800 MB/s in Express mode, roughly nine times the practical ceiling of a UHS-I card.

Is the $549.99 Mario Kart World bundle actually cheaper than buying separately?

Yes, by an exact $37.98. The parts at list price come to $587.97: a $499.99 console, the $79.99 Mario Kart World digital download, and a $7.99 three-month Nintendo Switch Online membership. At $549.99 the bundle is the only way Nintendo discounts the console right now. Just know the Mario Kart World copy is a download code with no cartridge, so you cannot resell it later.

Is $47.99 a good price for the Samsung P9 Express 256GB card?

It is a fair price in a volatile market but not the floor. Price trackers logged the card at $47.99 from late October through early November 2026, with a three-month average near $75 and a brief all-time low of $31.50 back in March. microSD Express supply has been erratic all year, so if $47.99 fits your budget, it is a reasonable buy. Patient buyers can set a price alert at $40 and see what Black Friday brings.

What is a Game-Key Card and should I avoid it?

A Game-Key Card is a physical cartridge that stores no game data, only a license. The full game downloads to the console when you insert the card. Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade, at 90GB, uses one, which means buying it also commits you to expansion storage. The cards are not a scam, but they erase the usual physical-media advantages: insert the cart and you still need the download. If reselling or lending games matters to you, check which Switch 2 releases use this format before buying.

Will the Switch 2 console itself get cheaper for Black Friday 2026?

History says no. Nintendo raised the console price from $449.99 to $499.99 on September 1, and the bare console has not tracked below either number. The bundles are the discount valve: $529.99 with Nintendo Switch Sports Resort or $549.99 with Mario Kart World. Those bundles sold out repeatedly during fall 2025, so if a bundle is in stock at list, that is the deal.
