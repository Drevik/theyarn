## What to Look for in a Backpacking Stove

Weight, boil time, and fuel efficiency are the three metrics that matter most. A 2024 review in Backpacker Magazine tested 27 stoves and found that canister stoves boil water 40 percent faster than liquid fuel stoves on average.

## Top Picks

### 1. MSR PocketRocket 2

The MSR PocketRocket 2 weighs just 2.6 ounces and brings one liter of water to a boil in 3.5 minutes at full power. The serrated pot supports hold small pots securely, and the built-in wind clip protects the flame in light wind. MSR includes a hard-shell carrying case.

**Price:** $49.95
**Weight:** 2.6 oz
**Fuel type:** Canister

### 2. BRS-3000T

The BRS-3000T is the lightest canister stove on the market at 0.9 ounces. It costs less than $20 and performs well for short trips in mild conditions. The pot supports are narrow, which limits it to smaller pots. Over 8,000 Amazon reviewers rate it 4.4 out of 5 stars.

**Price:** $18.99
**Weight:** 0.9 oz
**Fuel type:** Canister

### 3. MSR WhisperLite Universal

The WhisperLite Universal runs on both canister fuel and liquid fuels including white gas and kerosene. It is the best choice for cold-weather trips and international travel where canister fuel may not be available. The self-cleaning Shaker Jet simplifies maintenance on long trips.

**Price:** $149.95
**Weight:** 11.5 oz
**Fuel type:** Canister and liquid fuel

## How to Choose

For weekend trips in normal conditions, a lightweight canister stove like the PocketRocket 2 is the best balance of weight and performance. For ultralight hikers counting every gram, the BRS-3000T at under one ounce is hard to beat. For cold weather or international travel, the WhisperLite Universal handles any fuel type you can find.
