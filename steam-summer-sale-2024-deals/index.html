Steam Summer Sale 2024: Best PC Gaming Deals & Hardware Picks

June 27, 2024 | 3 Products Reviewed

Look, I've been staring at silicon and pixels since the days of 486 processors and much louder fans. I don't care about the fancy light shows or the "gamer" aesthetics that cost an extra $50 for essentially the same capacitors. I care about one thing: value. Specifically, how many hours of entertainment am I getting for every dollar I pull out of my wallet?

The Steam Summer Sale is officially live, and while the storefront is a sea of bright colors and flashing banners designed to trigger your dopamine receptors, most of it is noise. You don't need a 20% discount on a game you’ll play for twenty minutes. You need the deep cuts—the titles that offer massive longevity and the hardware that actually moves the needle on your experience.

I've combed through the current madness to find the software and hardware that won't leave you feeling like you've been hit by a microtransaction. Let's get into the actual math of the sale.

🏆 Best Overall

Steam Deck OLED

  • Stunning HDR-capable OLED display
  • Improved battery life over the original LCD model
  • Exceptional price-per-performance for handheld gaming

If you want to take your library on the go without being tethered to a desk, this is the gold standard. The screen is a massive upgrade, making everything from indie pixels to AAA textures pop. It's expensive, sure, but the longevity of the hardware justifies the initial hit to the bank account.

Downside: The price premium over the LCD version is steep if you're purely chasing the lowest entry cost.

Elden Ring

  • Massive, challenging open world
  • Incredible depth and replayability
  • Significant discount during the summer window

FromSoftware knows how to build worlds that respect your intelligence. At $42, this isn't just a sale; it's a steal. You can sink hundreds of hours into the Lands Between without ever feeling like the game is holding your hand.

Downside: It's punishing. If you're looking for something low-stress after a long workday, look elsewhere.

Check Latest Price

Fallout 76

  • Post-apocalyptic exploration
  • Massive player base and frequent updates
  • Deep discount (under $10)

For ten bucks, you get a wasteland that's surprisingly robust. Whether you're soloing mutated monsters or coordinating with a squad, the value here is objectively undeniable. It's a great way to pick up a big-budget title without any real financial risk.

Downside: The server-dependent nature means you're at the mercy of Bethesda's infrastructure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I buy the Steam Deck LCD or OLED?

If you have the budget, OLED. If you are trying to squeeze every cent of value out of your setup, the LCD version is still a beast.

Is Elden Ring worth the price jump from the base sale?

At $42, it's a non-negotiable buy for any PC enthusiast.

How long does the Steam Summer Sale last?

It typically runs through mid-July. Don't wait; the best deals rotate.

The Verdict

If you want the best single investment this summer, grab the Steam Deck OLED. It transforms how you access your entire library.
