Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Data Controller

The Yarn (theyarnreview.com) is the data controller for the limited information described in this policy. For any privacy-related inquiries, please reach out through our about page.

Information We Collect

The Yarn collects minimal information. Specifically:

  • Analytics data: We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This includes anonymized page views, referral sources, device types, and country-level location data. Cloudflare Analytics does not use client-side cookies or track you across other websites. Data is retained for 30 days and then automatically deleted.
  • Affiliate link clicks: When you click an affiliate link, you are directed to Amazon.com, which has its own Privacy Notice. We encourage you to review Amazon's privacy practices.
  • Server logs: Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may automatically collect standard technical information like IP addresses, browser type, and timestamps for security and performance purposes. This data is not used for marketing or sold to third parties.

We do not collect: names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, or any other personally identifiable information directly.

How We Use Your Information

  • To understand which content is most helpful to our readers
  • To improve site performance and user experience
  • To credit affiliate referrals through Amazon's tracking system
  • To maintain site security and prevent abuse

Cookies

The Yarn uses minimal cookies. For full details on what cookies we and third parties set, see our Cookie Policy.

Third-Party Links

Our site contains links to Amazon.com and other third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before sharing any personal information.

Data Retention

  • Analytics data: 30 days, then automatically deleted
  • Server logs: Retained for up to 7 days for security purposes, then purged
  • No personal data: We do not maintain databases of personal information beyond session-level analytics

Your Rights

GDPR Rights (European Economic Area)

Even though The Yarn is primarily a US-based site, we respect the privacy rights of all visitors, including those protected by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If you are in the EEA, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Data portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object: Object to the processing of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw any previously given consent at any time

Since we collect minimal analytics data through Cloudflare (which is aggregated and anonymized by default), most of these rights are already satisfied by our data practices. To exercise any of these rights, contact us through our about page.

CCPA Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know: Request disclosure of what personal information is collected, how it's used, and with whom it's shared
  • Delete: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt-out of sale: Direct businesses not to sell your personal information. The Yarn does not sell personal information — this right is provided for transparency
  • Non-discrimination: Exercise your CCPA rights without being discriminated against

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. The only data sharing occurs through Amazon's affiliate tracking when you click an affiliate link, which is governed by Amazon's privacy policy.

Do Not Track

We honor Do Not Track (DNT) browser signals. If your browser sends a DNT signal, Cloudflare Analytics will not track your visit. See our Cookie Policy for instructions on enabling DNT in your browser.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any information we collect. Our site is served over HTTPS, uses Cloudflare's security features (including DDoS protection and web application firewall), and enforces strict security headers. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

International Users

The Yarn is hosted in the United States. If you are accessing our site from outside the US, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States. By using our site, you consent to this transfer. We maintain the same privacy standards for all visitors regardless of location.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, wish to exercise your data rights, or need to report a privacy concern, please reach out through our about page.
