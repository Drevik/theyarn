What Actually Matters When Hunting For Deals

Before the picks, here's what separates a good choice from a waste of money:

  • Check the PS Plus Tiers: Don't just look at the sale price. Sometimes, a game included in your Premium or Extra subscription is better value than any $10 deal you find elsewhere. Always check the service catalogue first!
  • Genre Longevity Over Graphics: A great narrative RPG that has 40+ hours of content for $15 will always beat a graphically stunning, but short, single-session blockbuster. Look at playtime estimates, not just trailers.
  • Sale Timing Matters: The absolute best prices rarely happen when the game launches. Wait for seasonal sales (Black Friday, summer clearances) or check third-party reseller sites like Discogs for physical copies of older titles.
  • The 'Free DLC' Trap: Be wary of a low price because the game is incomplete. Always verify if major expansions, multiplayer components, or critical story arcs are included in that $20 sticker price.

Our Top Picks

🏆 Best Overall

Marvel's Spider-Man: Miles Morales — Best Overall

When I first played Miles, I was blown away by how much pure joy it delivered. Swinging through New York City is genuinely one of the best gaming experiences I’ve had in years; the traversal mechanics feel so fluid and responsive that they almost become a sport. The combat system keeps you constantly engaged with satisfying web-swinging combos and enemy takedowns, making every corner worth exploring.

The story here is personal and hits hard—it feels like a perfect sequel without overshadowing the fantastic core gameplay loop of swinging and fighting. My only minor gripe is that since it's such an action-focused game, the deep character development sometimes takes a backseat to immediate spectacle. But honestly, for a deal under $20, this title provides 95% of a AAA masterpiece.

Best for: The perfect blend of cinematic story and unmatched web-slinging fun.

🏆 Best Budget Pick

Sackboy: A Big Adventure — Best Budget Pick

I grabbed this during a sale purely because it was cheap, expecting maybe some repetitive platforming. I was wrong—this game is pure, unadulterated creative fun! It captures the whimsical spirit of classic PlayStation titles while modernizing it beautifully for PS5's haptics. The levels are incredibly inventive; one section involved navigating giant yarn ball traps, which was hilarious to play through.

It’s a brilliant co-op experience if you have friends available, but even solo, the gameplay variety keeps things fresh. If I had one criticism, it's that some of the later levels can feel slightly overly complex just to force the player into a specific puzzle solution. Still, for this price point, it offers endless hours of joyful exploration and is unmatched family fun.

Best for: Families and players who want pure, creative platforming joy without breaking the bank.

🏆 Best for Open-World RPG Fans

Horizon Zero Dawn Complete Edition — Best for Open-World RPG Fans

If you love massive open worlds with a deep lore and satisfying combat, this is your champion. The sheer scale of the world—roaming through lush valleys while dodging gigantic mechanical dinosaurs—is breathtaking. Aloy is an incredible protagonist, and the mystery surrounding the 'Old Ones' keeps me hooked for dozens of hours. Combat-wise, tracking down specific machine weak points feels incredibly tactical and rewarding.

The biggest draw here, besides the gorgeous PS5 visuals, is the world-building; every scrap of dialogue or ruin tells a story. My only downside is that the initial learning curve can be steep; you might feel overwhelmed by the amount of gear and lore presented right away. But once you get your bearings, it's an absolute powerhouse experience.

Best for: Explorers who crave massive open worlds and satisfying boss battles.

🏆 Best Technical Showcase

Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart — Best Technical Showcase

This game is less about the story (though it’s fun!) and more about showing off what this PS5 hardware can do, and boy, does it deliver. The instantaneous dimensional travel when you zip between planets is genuinely mind-boggling—it's a technical marvel that makes all my previous console experiences feel quaint by comparison. The combat is wild; the variety of weapons and gadgets means every fight feels like an over-the-top cartoon spectacle.

The polish on this game is impeccable, from the vibrant particle effects to the seamless loading times. However, because it's so focused on showcasing hardware power, some of the character interactions feel a bit lightweight compared to titles like Spider-Man. If you are buying this specifically for its technical prowess and adrenaline rush, it’s worth every penny.

Best for: Gamers who want to experience the absolute pinnacle of PS5 graphical capability.

🏆 Best for Stylish Action/Martial Arts

Sifu — Best for Stylish Action/Martial Arts

If you are a fan of highly choreographed, martial arts combat—think Jackie Chan meets brutal Tokyo nightlife—you need to play Sifu. The core loop is simple: get beaten up and get stronger. This mechanic adds incredible weight and consequence to every fight; you genuinely feel like your character is improving as he gets older. The takedowns are visceral, making even a simple street brawl feel incredibly impactful.

The combat system rewards aggressive play and mastery of the various fighting styles, which was highly satisfying to learn. My primary complaint, though, is that the camera can sometimes be overly aggressive during intense brawls, occasionally dipping into my personal space for dramatic effect. It’s a niche gem, but if you enjoy perfecting your combos and surviving brutal fights, this is an absolute must-buy.

Best for: Combat purists who crave stylish, combo-heavy martial arts action.
