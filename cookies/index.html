Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, understand how you use the site, and improve your experience. Cookies are standard practice across the web and are used by nearly every site you visit.

Cookies We Use

Analytics Cookies

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our site. Cloudflare's analytics are privacy-respecting:

  • What's collected: Anonymized page views, referral sources, device types, and country-level location data
  • What's not collected: Personal identifiers, names, email addresses, or browsing history across other sites
  • Cookie-free tracking: Cloudflare Analytics does not use client-side cookies or localStorage for tracking — it relies on aggregated, server-side data
  • Data retention: Analytics data is retained for 30 days, then automatically deleted

Cloudflare Analytics is designed to comply with privacy regulations without requiring a consent banner. You can learn more at cloudflare.com/web-analytics.

Affiliate Tracking Cookies

When you click an affiliate link on The Yarn (such as a link to Amazon), the destination site may set its own cookies on your browser. These are third-party cookies set by the retailer, not by us.

For Amazon specifically:

  • Amazon sets a cookie when you click an affiliate link so they can track the referral and credit any qualifying purchase to our account
  • This cookie is set by Amazon.com and is subject to Amazon's Privacy Notice
  • We do not have access to or control over Amazon's cookies

Essential Cookies

The Yarn currently does not use any essential cookies (such as login sessions or shopping carts). Our site is fully static and functional without any cookies.

Managing Cookies

You can control, block, or delete cookies through your browser settings. Here's how to access cookie settings in common browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

Please note that blocking all cookies may affect site functionality, though The Yarn is designed to work well even with cookies disabled.

Do Not Track

We honor Do Not Track (DNT) browser signals. If your browser sends a DNT signal, Cloudflare Analytics will not track your visit. You can enable DNT in your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Requires an extension (such as Privacy Badger)
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → "Send websites a 'Do Not Track' signal"
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → "Ask websites not to track me"
