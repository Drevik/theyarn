Product Copy (Sales Page)

Pacing Pulse

Stop Guessing. Start Measuring.

Your story sags. You know it does. You just don't know where — or how to fix it.

Every writer has been there: the first act zips along, the climax delivers, but the middle? It drags. The tension flatlines. Readers start skimming. You've heard the advice — "vary your sentence length," "cut the filler," "raise the stakes" — but none of it tells you where your pacing breaks or what specifically to change.

Pacing Pulse fixes that.

This isn't another writing advice book. It's a diagnostic system — a set of scorecards, worksheets, and maps that give you a number for every scene's tension level. When you can measure pacing, you can fix it.

What You'll Get

  • The Pacing Diagnostic System — Identify exactly which type of pacing problem your story has (there are only 6)
  • Tension Scorecards — Rate every scene on 4 dimensions. See your story's rhythm at a glance
  • Tension Curve Mapper — Plot your entire story's tension arc. Saggy middles become visually obvious
  • The Fix Library — 24 specific pacing fixes organized by problem type. Not theory — actions
  • Genre Pacing Guides — Thriller, romance, fantasy, and literary fiction all pace differently. Know what your genre expects
  • Quick Reference Card — One page you can print and keep beside your keyboard

How It Works

  1. Score — Rate each scene's tension, stakes, momentum, and surprise (2 minutes per scene)
  2. Map — Plot your scores on the tension curve (10 minutes for a novel)
  3. Diagnose — The curve tells you exactly what's wrong (flat sections = sag, spikes with no setup = rushed)
  4. Fix — Open the Fix Library to the matching problem type and apply the specific solution

Total time to diagnose a full novel: 30-45 minutes.

FAQ

Do I need to have a finished draft? No — you can score outlined scenes too. Pacing Pulse works for plotters and pantsers.

Is this for fiction only? Yes. This system is designed for narrative fiction (novels, novellas, short stories). It's not for nonfiction or screenplays (though screenwriters may find the tension concepts useful).

How is this different from Scene Doctor? Scene Doctor diagnoses broken scenes. Pacing Pulse diagnoses broken rhythm across scenes. Use Scene Doctor on individual scenes that don't work. Use Pacing Pulse when your scenes work individually but the story still feels slow.

Can I use this with Scrivener/Google Docs/Word? Yes — the scorecards are designed to work alongside whatever you write in. Print them or fill them digitally.
