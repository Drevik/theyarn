Final Fantasy VII: Complete Strategy Guide

August 2026 · PS1 · RPG

Welcome to Final Fantasy VII: A Classic That Time Can't Erase

Welcome back, gaming aficionados! If you're venturing into the world of Final Fantasy VII for the first time or are looking to polish your skills on this classic PS1 gem, you've come to the right place. The game that defined an era—now, let's dive in and explore why Final Fantasy VII remains one of the most beloved RPGs ever made.

Why Final Fantasy VII Stands Out

First off, what makes Final Fantasy VII a must-play for anyone who loves RPGs? Well, it’s not just about the epic storyline or the stunning visuals; it's also the gameplay that blends seamlessly with its narrative. The game is known for its deep character development and world-building, which you can experience through varied combat systems, intricate dialogue choices, and a richly detailed story.

The game introduces a unique blend of turn-based battles mixed with real-time action, making every encounter feel dynamic and engaging. Plus, the music... oh, let's not forget about the iconic score by Nobuo Uematsu. Each track is a masterpiece that complements the emotional beats of the story perfectly.

The Story in Brief

Now, for a quick rundown of what you can expect from Final Fantasy VII's narrative without giving away any major spoilers:

  • The Prologue: Cloud Strife joins Shinra, the corrupt megacorporation, but soon becomes disillusioned.
  • Emerging Threats: A mysterious figure, Angeal Hewley and his partner Genesis Rhapsodos, are on a mission to uncover the truth behind Shinra's misdeeds.
  • The Lifestream: The world’s energy source is connected to a cycle that involves the planet's life force. This concept drives much of the game's deeper themes.
  • Emergence of Sephiroth: A powerful and enigmatic villain emerges, driving the plot towards an epic showdown.

Core Controls Reference

Before we get into the nitty-gritty, let’s cover some essential controls:

#### Movement

  • Use the D-Pad to move your party members around.
  • Press Start to pause the game at any time.

#### Battle Mechanics

  • Battle Menu: When in battle, press Square (B button) to open the menu. From here, you can choose your actions:
- Attack: Tap X (Triangle on PC).

- Magic: Tap Y (Square on PC).

- Item: Press R1.

- Skill: Hold L1 and then tap X, Y, or B.

#### Item Usage

  • To use an item, select it from the menu and press R1 to confirm.

Key Items List

Here’s a list of essential items you’ll want to collect throughout your journey:

1. Materia:

- Ice Materia: For dealing ice damage.

- Fire Materia: For fire-based attacks.

- Lightning Materia: Enhances physical and magic attack power.

- Air Materia: Increases evasion.

2. Potion/Hi-Potion: Basic healing items for minor injuries.

3. Antidote: To heal status ailments like poison or paralysis.

4. Phoenix Down: A one-time use item that fully restores HP and MP, useful when you really need a break from battle.

5. Flare Tonics: For using Flare spells, which can be very powerful.

Game Progression Roadmap

Now, let’s map out your journey through Final Fantasy VII with a step-by-step guide to help you make the most of your experience:

1. Start at Nibelheim and the Beginning:

- Begin by exploring Cloud's hometown, Nibelheim. Collect items like Materia and fight off enemies.

2. Join Up with Aerith and Barrett:

- Head towards the Shinra HQ in Midgar to start building your party and gain new members.

3. Learn from The Battle of Aerith’s Death:

- This is a crucial battle where you must defeat Sephiroth before he takes Aerith away. It's not just about the fight; it sets up a pivotal moment for the story.

4. Explore Corel and Train at Junon:

- Head to Corel for more Materia and battles, then train your party in the safe haven of Junon. This will prepare you for tougher challenges ahead.

5. Engage with the Turk Team:

- Follow Cloud's journey through the Turks' headquarters in CP Tower. The battles here are intense but rewarding.

6. Return to Nibelheim and Face Angeal and Genesis:

- This is a key battle that reveals more about Sephiroth’s past and strengthens your party with powerful abilities.

7. Reach the Shinra Headquarters and Confront Jenova:

- After setting up your team, head towards Shinra HQ in Midgar for a showdown. This fight sets the stage for the final confrontation.

With this roadmap, you’ll be well-prepared to face the challenges that lie ahead. Remember, every member of your party plays a crucial role, so make sure to balance their skills and Materia accordingly. And don’t forget to save often—nothing is more annoying than dying at a critical moment!

Wrapping Up

That’s it for our opening section! Whether you’re starting fresh or revisiting Final Fantasy VII, I hope this guide helps you make the most of your adventures in Midgar and beyond. Happy gaming, brave warriors!

The Early Game Dungeons of Final Fantasy VII (PS1)

Midegar Sector 5 Slums

#### Key Items:

  • Materia: Air Elemental, Fire Elemental
  • Potion
  • Ethers X2

#### Puzzle Solutions and Enemy Encounters:

Room 1: Entrance Hall
  • Enemies: Goblins
  • Strategy: These enemies are weak but come in groups. Use magic or a high-level attack to clear them out quickly.
Room 2: Corridor
  • Enemies: Slithers
  • Strategy: Be cautious; Slithers can be tricky with their poison attacks. Use healing items if necessary.
Room 3: Warehouse
  • Key Item: Materia - Air Elemental
  • Puzzle: Push the lever in front of you to open a secret passage.
  • Enemies: Munchkins
  • Strategy: These enemies have low HP and are easy to defeat with basic attacks. Use Air Elemental if you have it.
Room 4: Stairway
  • Key Item: Potion
  • Puzzle: There is no puzzle here; simply navigate the stairs carefully.
  • Enemies: Goblins, Slithers
  • Strategy: Follow the pattern for previous rooms and use potions as needed.

Nibelheim

#### Key Items:

  • Materia: Fire Elemental, Ice Elemental
  • Potion
  • Ethers X2

#### Puzzle Solutions and Enemy Encounters:

Room 1: Entrance Hall
  • Enemies: Goblins
  • Strategy: As before, these are weak and can be dispatched easily with magic or melee attacks.
Room 2: Living Room
  • Key Item: Materia - Fire Elemental
  • Puzzle: Use the fireplace to heat up the floor tiles. The correct sequence is South, North, East.
  • Enemies: Slithers
  • Strategy: Take your time and use potions if you get hit.
Room 3: Kitchen
  • Key Item: Potion
  • Puzzle: Open the fridge by placing the key under the magnet on the door. Inside, read the note.
  • Enemies: Munchkins
  • Strategy: These enemies are weak; focus fire to take them down quickly.
Room 4: Bedroom
  • Key Item: Materia - Ice Elemental
  • Puzzle: Activate the water heater by pushing the button in front of it. Use the ice crystal to freeze a keyhole, then unlock the door.
  • Enemies: Slithers
  • Strategy: Ice attacks can be highly effective here; use them when they are encased in ice.

Rocket Town

#### Key Items:

  • Materia: Thunder Elemental, Water Elemental
  • Potion
  • Ethers X2

#### Puzzle Solutions and Enemy Encounters:

Room 1: Entrance Hall
  • Enemies: Goblins
  • Strategy: Use basic attacks to clear them quickly.
Room 2: Control Room
  • Key Item: Materia - Thunder Elemental
  • Puzzle: Open the computer terminal to access the data. The correct sequence is Blue, Red, Yellow.
  • Enemies: Slithers
  • Strategy: Be careful; these enemies can be tricky with their poison attacks.
Room 3: Cargo Bay
  • Key Item: Potion
  • Puzzle: Use the crane to lift the crate and place it on the platform. This opens a secret passage.
  • Enemies: Munchkins, Goblins
  • Strategy: Clear out the mooks and use potions if needed.
Room 4: Office
  • Key Item: Materia - Water Elemental
  • Puzzle: Solve the riddle by pushing the button in front of the door three times.
  • Enemies: Slithers, Goblins
  • Strategy: Ice attacks can be particularly effective here. Use them to freeze enemies and then attack.

Summary

Each of these early-game dungeons serves as a training ground for your party. The key items you find—specifically the Elemental Materia—will significantly boost your abilities in elemental magic, making future battles easier. Be sure to use potions and ethers liberally, especially when facing tougher enemies like Slithers.

The puzzles in each dungeon are straightforward but require attention to detail. Pay close attention to the sequence of tiles or buttons that need to be activated; these will reward you with valuable items or open secret passages.

By mastering these early dungeons, you'll not only gather essential Materia and healing items but also develop a better understanding of enemy patterns and combat strategies.

Late Game Dungeons: A Comprehensive Strategy Guide for Final Fantasy VII (PS1)

As you approach the final stages of your journey with Cloud Strife, Tifa Lockhart, and Aerith Gainsborough, the battles grow more intense, and the stakes higher. This guide will cover essential strategies for tackling the late-game dungeons in Final Fantasy VII: Nibelheim, Plateau Reactor, and Rufus Shinra's Office. We’ll delve into key item requirements, puzzle solutions, mini-boss strategies, and main boss patterns with detailed information on dodging and damage phases.

Nibelheim

#### Key Items

  • Elixir: Essential for restoring HP to all party members.
  • Phoenix Down: For quick recovery of a single character’s HP.
  • Materia: Aim for Protect and Shell, which are particularly useful against high-elemental attacks.
  • Mega-Potion and Mega-Ether: For the boss battles, where healing will be crucial.

#### Puzzle Solutions

The Nibelheim dungeon involves several puzzles that require precise timing. The key to success is patience and careful planning:

1. First Floor:

- Navigate through the maze-like corridors by using Wall Materia on the walls.

- Collect the necessary keys from locked doors before proceeding.

2. Second Floor:

- Use the Reflect Materia to avoid taking damage from the traps.

- The puzzles here involve moving between mirrors and avoiding falling into pits. Keep an eye on your map to avoid getting lost.

3. Third Floor:

- Pay attention to the door patterns that require specific key combinations. For example, press a key at certain intervals to open doors.

- Use Erase Materia to remove some of the walls for easier passage if needed.

4. Final Boss: Sephiroth

#### Mini-Boss Strategy

  • Puzzle Room: The room containing the mini-bosses is filled with moving platforms and traps.
- Approach slowly, using Protect or Shell Materia to mitigate damage.

- Be cautious of the falling rocks and laser beams.

Plateau Reactor

#### Key Items

  • Mega-Potion: Essential for maintaining HP during intense battles.
  • Phoenix Down: Quick healing for single characters.
  • Materia: Aim for Protect, Shell, and Piercing.
  • Elixir: For complete recovery of the entire party.

#### Puzzle Solutions

The Plateau Reactor has a series of puzzles that involve both timed actions and strategic thinking:

1. First Floor:

- Use the map to find hidden pathways and keys.

- Avoid the moving platforms by timing your jumps carefully.

2. Second Floor:

- Solve the puzzle involving the pressure plates and levers to open a door.

- Pay attention to the directions of the arrows and move accordingly.

3. Third Floor:

- Navigate through the maze, using Wall Materia on the walls for protection.

- Collect keys from locked doors before proceeding further.

4. Final Boss: Nibelhiem Reactor

#### Mini-Boss Strategy

  • Barghest: In this area, you’ll face multiple Barghests in rapid succession.
- Use Protect and Shell to minimize damage.

- Focus on the barghest with the most health; defeating it first will make subsequent battles easier.

Rufus Shinra's Office

#### Key Items

  • Mega-Potion: For maintaining HP during intense boss battles.
  • Phoenix Down: Quick healing for single characters.
  • Materia: Aim for Protect, Shell, and Piercing.
  • Elixir: For complete recovery of the entire party.

#### Puzzle Solutions

The Rufus Shinra's Office area is relatively straightforward but requires careful planning:

1. First Floor:

- Use the map to navigate through the office, finding keys for locked doors.

- Avoid triggering traps by moving carefully and using Erase Materia if needed.

2. Second Floor:

- Solve the puzzle involving the levers and pressure plates to open a door.

- Follow the directions on the ground to avoid falling into pits.

3. Third Floor:

- Use Wall Materia for protection against random attacks.

- Collect keys from locked doors before proceeding further.

4. Final Boss: Rufus Shinra

#### Main Boss Strategies

##### Sephiroth

  • Patterns and Dodges:
- Sephiroth’s primary attack is a sword slash, which can be dodged by moving to the side just as he swings his blade.

- His Ultimate Limit attacks include Overdrive, which can be interrupted with quick attacks or Piercing Materia.

- Use Protect and Shell to minimize damage during his final forms.

##### Nibelhiem Reactor

  • Patterns and Dodges:
- The reactor has a series of mechanical gears that rotate and emit projectiles. Dodge by moving away from them just as they start spinning.

- Focus on the boss’s weak points, which are often indicated by specific attacks or phases.

- Use Shell to avoid taking too much damage.

##### Rufus Shinra

  • Patterns and Dodges:
- Rufus uses a variety of spells and physical attacks. Use Protect and Shell Materia for protection.

- His Ultimate Limit attack can be dodged by moving out of the area just as he initiates it.

- Avoid his long-range attacks by staying close to the walls or using Wall Materia.

By mastering these strategies, you’ll be well-prepared to face the final challenges in Final Fantasy VII. Remember that patience and careful planning are key to success in these late-game dungeons. Good luck on your journey!

Guard Scorpion

Guard Scorpion is a challenging early boss that tests your reflexes and timing. Aim for level 12 and equip the Teleport Materia. After its initial attack, focus on dodging its tail swipes by moving in quick bursts to avoid damage. Use Quick Attack or similar techniques to keep it at bay while delivering weak hits.

Reno

Reno fights with both a sword and fire-based magic. Aim for level 18 and bring the Fire Materia. He switches between physical attacks and magic, so use Protect to mitigate incoming damage from his spells. When he stops casting, take advantage of the pause to land critical hits with your sword or magic.

Air Buster

Air Buster is a formidable air-based enemy that uses high-damage breaths. Aim for level 16 and equip the Reflect Materia. Stay on the ground during its aerial attacks; when it descends, reflect its breath back at it using the Magic command. Use Thundaga to deal significant damage while avoiding its Fire Breath.

Sample:H0512

Sample:H0512 is a genetic experiment with high defense and massive HP. Aim for level 20 and equip Moxie to boost your party’s stats. Focus on quick, precise attacks using weapons like the Scimitar or Sickle. When it switches between modes, avoid its special attacks; then, target its weak points while it is vulnerable.

Rufus

Rufus fights with a focus on magic and has high evasion. Aim for level 26 and equip Holy Materia to deliver powerful spells. Use the Protect and Shell commands early to mitigate his magic damage. As he switches between phases, quickly adapt your strategy—focus on critical hits in his weaker forms while using status effects and attacks to weaken him before his final phase.

These strategies ensure you can handle these bosses with a balanced approach, leveraging appropriate levels and Materia to maximize effectiveness.

Collectibles and Secrets in Final Fantasy VII (PS1)

Welcome to our comprehensive guide on collecting every heart piece, container, bottle, trading sequences, spells/songs, hidden areas, and minigame strategies in Final Fantasy VII for the PlayStation 1. Whether you're a veteran revisiting old territory or a newcomer eager to uncover all the hidden gems, this section will ensure you don’t miss out on anything.

Heart Pieces and Containers

#### Area: Midgar

  • Heart Container (Shinra Tower): Located in Shinra HQ’s upper levels, after defeating Sephiroth. Use the elevator to reach the top.
  • Heart Piece 1: In the slums, go to the lower level of the market, where Tifa works. Talk to her and follow the sequence.
  • Heart Piece 2: After acquiring the Materia Scanner from Aerith, visit the slum’s northwestern sector. Enter the small alley with stairs leading down; the heart piece is inside a bottle on the ground.

#### Area: Nibelheim

  • Heart Container (Church): Inside the church, after battling the enemy. Use the key item to open the secret door and defeat the boss.
  • Heart Piece 1: In the house where Tifa’s father once lived, search behind the desk in the study room.
  • Heart Piece 2: In the Nibelheim sewers. Go down from the area where you fight the enemies, then use a key card to access the secret passage and collect the heart piece.

#### Area: Junon

  • Heart Piece (Junon Beach): Found after defeating the boss in Junon Harbor. Use the key item to open the chest containing the heart piece.

Bottles and Inventory Expansions

#### Area: Nibelheim

  • Bottles: In the house behind Tifa’s father, there are two bottles in the study room. The first one is on a shelf; the second requires you to use the Materia Scanner to reveal it under the desk.

#### Area: Plateau of Bahamut

  • Inventory Expansion (Blue Key): After defeating Bahamut, access the secret area behind the rock formation with the key item. Inside, find the Blue Key.

Trading Sequences

#### Area: Midgar

  • Trading Sequence 1: Visit the market and talk to Tifa in her house. Give her the flower she asks for, and she will give you a Materia.

#### Area: Nibelheim

  • Trading Sequence 2: Return to Nibelheim when the village is destroyed. Talk to Aerith at the church; she will request a key item, which you can acquire through various means.

Spells, Songs, and Special Abilities

#### Area: Shinra Tower

  • Spells (Aerith): After defeating Sephiroth in the Shinra Tower, head back down. Speak with Aerith to unlock her spells: Cura, Esuna, and Phoenix Down.

#### Area: Nibelheim Church

  • Songs (Tifa): Defeat the boss in the church and return to Tifa’s house. She will teach you songs that can be used in battle or during cutscenes.

Hidden Areas and Secrets

#### Area: Midgar

  • Hidden Area 1: Accessible via a secret passage behind the market. Use the key card to open it, leading to a hidden area with a heart piece and bottles.

#### Area: Nibelheim

  • Secret Passages: Explore the sewers after defeating the enemies; they lead to additional areas with collectibles and heart pieces. Also, revisit Aerith’s house before fighting Sephiroth for hidden bottles and items.

Minigame Strategies

#### Chocobo Racing Minigame:

1. Track Selection: Choose tracks that match your Chocobo's stats.

2. Speed Tips:

- Use the Turbo Boost button wisely; it can be used once per race.

- Utilize the brake to slow down and regain speed at strategic points.

- Practice on easier courses before attempting harder ones.

#### Materia Locking:

1. Materia Combination: Experiment with different Materia combinations to find synergies that enhance your party’s abilities.

2. Unlocking Materia: Use the Unlock command to add new abilities to your materia slots. This requires Materia Shards which can be found in various dungeons and battles.

#### Battle Strategies:

1. Materia Utilization: Prioritize equipping Materia based on the enemy type you encounter. For example, use Ice Materia against Fire-based enemies.

2. Skill Synergy: Combine abilities that complement each other to maximize damage output or healing effects.

By exploring these hidden areas and utilizing all your available resources, you can ensure a complete and rewarding experience in Final Fantasy VII. Happy collecting!

Inventory/Equipment Guide

Essential Items

  • Potion: Basic healing item.
  • Elixir: Restores all HP & MP to full.
  • Materia: Enhances character abilities.

Recommended Equipment

  • Armor: Protects against physical attacks. Consider high defense pieces like the Nibelheim Vest and Gold Cloak.
  • Weapons: Choose weapons with high attack power, e.g., Masamune or Buster Sword for Cloud.
  • Accessories: Provide additional buffs. Helmets often have good effects.

All Upgrades and Locations

Crisis Core - Final Fantasy VII

  • Materia Slots: Maximize slots by using the Triple Triad cards to unlock fourth slot.
  • Skill Materia: Upgrade characters with necessary skills for easier battles.
  • Armor Materia: Enhance armor with special abilities like Overdrive or Haste.

Main Game Locations

  • Nibelheim: Complete sidequests; receive important items and upgrades.
  • Rocket Town: Collect rare materials needed for high-level equipment.
  • Edge: Access the Shinra Tower; get key plot items from Cid Highwind.
  • Midgar: Visit Sector 7 Slums, Upper Plate, and Lower Garbage Dump for additional Materia and weapons.

Speedrun Basics

Key Glitches Explained Simply

1. Time Freeze:

- Use the "Pause" button to freeze time in battle and perform multiple actions quickly.

2. Instant Kill:

- Combine this with the Overkill glitch (defeating enemies with high damage) for quick boss battles.

Version Differences

PS1 vs. Remaster

  • PS1: Limited graphics, no auto-targeting, and slower combat.
  • Remaster: Improved graphics, auto-targeting, enhanced difficulty settings, and more accessible menus.

FAQ Section

Q: How do I get the Overkill glitch?

  • A: Equip high-level weapons with high attack power. Defeat an enemy that does not have enough HP to kill you in one hit. Once the damage is over 90%, use a weak attack on the enemy to instantly kill it.

Q: Can I play through Crisis Core without completing the main game?

  • A: Yes, but key items and Materia are required for later chapters. Completing parts of the main game will make your journey easier in Crisis Core.

Q: What’s the best way to farm for Gelnika materials?

  • A: Focus on areas where Gelnika is common, like Rocket Town or the Nibel Mountains. Use the "Find" command during exploration and revisit locations with high spawns.

Q: How do I defeat Sephiroth in Crisis Core?

  • A: Use Cloud’s Limit Breaks (Overdrive) to quickly reduce his HP. Focus on using attacks that cause status effects, especially the Materia that provides Overkill.

Q: What are the benefits of completing all Side Missions in Crisis Core?

  • A: You get additional Materia and equipment drops. Completing all Side Missions also unlocks a special ending for each character, providing more insight into their roles and motivations.
